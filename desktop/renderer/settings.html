Syndicates dock

A dock in your colours replaces the look of the taskbar: Home, pinned apps, open windows and the clock. The Windows taskbar hides itself while the dock is on.

Pin apps by right-clicking a running app in the dock, or from Home's search (Pin button). Drag pinned icons to reorder them.

Windows can't animate the lock screen, so this uses a picture of your wallpaper with its widgets. The sign-in screen shows the same picture when “Show the lock screen background picture on the sign-in screen” is on in Windows lock screen settings.

Switch apps like a MacBook

Three-finger swipe left / right goes straight to the previous / next app, without the Alt+Tab picker. Windows sends touchpad gestures to itself, so tell it once which keys a swipe should press:

  1. Open Touchpad settings → Advanced gestures.
  2. Under Three-finger gestures: Swipe left → Custom shortcut → press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+←; Swipe right → Ctrl+Alt+Shift+→.
  3. That's it: Syndicates switches instantly on those keys. They also work from the keyboard, and scrolling on the dock does the same.
