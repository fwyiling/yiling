Anime gallery

Anime wallpapers from Wallhaven, safe-for-work only, full HD or bigger. Click one to use it (for the desktop you're setting, when each has its own); it's kept, so it works offline too.

Dock

A dock along the bottom, like a Mac's: Home, your pinned apps, open windows, screenshots and the Bin. The Windows taskbar hides itself while the dock is on, and comes back when you turn it off or quit.

Pin apps by right-clicking a running app in the dock, or from Home's search (Pin button). Drag pinned icons to reorder them.

Island

Island petA little friend that lives in the island: it wanders about, naps when you're away, dances to music and cheers when a timer ends. Hover the island and click it to pet it; right-click it to meet the next one.

The island also shows volume and brightness changes, Caps Lock and headphones connecting. Ctrl+Alt+T opens its timer from anywhere.

Windows can't animate the lock screen, so this uses a picture of your wallpaper with its widgets. The sign-in screen shows the same picture when “Show the lock screen background picture on the sign-in screen” is on in Windows lock screen settings.

Themes

Themes restyle the dock, top bar, island, panels, Home, the desktops overview and the desktop widgets. Turn on several: where they overlap, the later one wins. Make your own here, or share one as a .sdtheme file: your friend imports it.

Desktops

Every desktop and its windows at a glance: Ctrl+Alt+Up, or the button next to the desktop numbers in the top bar.
Swipe between desktops like a MacBook

Windows has Spaces too: virtual desktops, with the same sliding animation. Three-finger swipe left / right moves between them.

  1. Click Use three-finger swipes for desktops below, or in Touchpad settings → Three-finger gestures → Swipes choose Switch desktops and show desktop.
  2. Make another desktop: Win+Ctrl+D, the top bar menu's New desktop, or swipe up and click New desktop.
  3. The top bar shows your desktops (1 2 3…); click one to go there. The top bar, dock and island are on every desktop.

Smoother switching: Windows is set to pick its accent colour from the wallpaper automatically, and it works that out again on every desktop switch, which can make the slide stutter. Syndicates covers the Windows wallpaper anyway, so choose Accent colour → Manual and pick one you like.

Switching apps instead

Ctrl+Alt+Shift+← / → (and scrolling on the dock) jump straight to the previous / next app.
