Visual references · draft 1

Lumenvale

A slow open-world game about wandering an island. You explore, gather, rest by fires and watch the light change, and other wanderers drift through. Below are three possible art directions, all painted live in code. Pick one, or mix them.

Direction A

Faceted Gold

Low-poly shapes with flat faces lit by warm, low sun. Clean and readable, and it runs smoothly in a browser even with huge view distances.

  • Hard-edged facets, soft gradient skies
  • Golden hour does most of the work
  • Long fog, so far ridges fade to lilac

Feels like: Alto's Odyssey, Journey, low-poly landscape art

Direction B

Cloudmeadow

Soft, rounded and painterly. Huge cumulus clouds, floating islands spilling waterfalls, and meadows full of flowers. The most cozy and hopeful of the three.

  • Rounded forms, almost no hard edges
  • Bright midday, pastel greens and sky blue
  • Wind in the grass, petals in the air

Feels like: hand-painted anime backgrounds, Sky: Children of the Light, Breath of the Wild

Direction C

Lumen Night

Dark and dreamy, where the world makes its own light. Aurora over the peaks, glowing crystals and mushrooms, and fireflies. The game would lean into its night half.

  • Deep blues with cyan and violet glow
  • Glowing things guide where you go
  • Mist banks and starfields

Feels like: Ori, Avatar's Pandora, Abzû

Places to find

Landmark studies. Each one is a reason to walk toward the horizon. Undiscovered places would show a faint pillar of light.

The Drifting Isles

Floating stones spiral up to islands you can stand on, then glide off.

The Sky Spire

A crystal on the highest peak. You can see it from anywhere at night.

The Elder Tree

A giant blossom tree with lanterns. Petals fall all day.

Old Watch Light

A lighthouse whose beam sweeps the sea after dusk.

One day on the island

The light cycles through a full day in about 12 real minutes. Resting by a fire skips ahead.

Morning08:00

What it should feel like

Light is the star

Every screenshot should look like a wallpaper. Sun angle, fog and glow carry the mood more than detail does.

Nothing chases you

No enemies, no timers, no health bar. You can sit on a cliff and watch the aurora for ten minutes.

Quietly alive

Wanderers walk the paths with name tags, say hello and post in a chat log. It feels like a populated MMO world, but at your own pace.
